What are IGCSEs?

IGCSE stands for International General Certificate of Secondary Education. It is a globally recognized school qualification for students aged 14 to 16.

Key Facts

  • Age group: Typically taken at the end of two years of study, usually around 16 years old (Grades 9-10 or Years 10-11).
  • Origin: Created as an international version of the UK's GCSE qualification.
  • Exam boards: Mainly offered by Cambridge International Education (CIE) and Pearson Edexcel.
  • Structure: Students take individual subjects rather than a single generalized test.
